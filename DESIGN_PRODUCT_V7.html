The problem

The argument is the thing you’re missing.

01
You have the documents.

The lease, the brief, the market notes, the sheet the finance team keeps. They are already there, and they are already paid for.

02
What is missing is the argument.

Not another meeting. A panel that reads the material, takes the positions it implies, and argues them against each other until one holds.

03
And a record you can check.

Every turn is filed with its position and its confidence. Nothing is summarised after the fact, so a claim can always be traced to the page it came from.

How it works

Four steps, and the last one is a document.

01

Add the material

PDF, DOCX, MD or TXT, kept in a material set. The panel reads each document once, at the page it was read from.

02

Ask the question

Write the decision as a question. The panel answers that sentence and nothing else. Choose the panel size and the passes.

03

The panel argues

Experts are seated from your material and argue in pairs, in passes. Each pass writes the weighted share of the four positions to the tape.

04

The answer is filed

One position, the confidence behind it, the case against, and every source. It opens as a document you can export.

The tape above is a worked example. It stops when one position holds 0.80 of the weight across the last two passes, or when the last pass ends.

What it does, and what it does not do

It is a panel, not an adviser.

It does three things

  • 01Read the material you gave it, and only that material.
  • 02Seat a panel from that material, and make it argue in passes.
  • 03File one position with its confidence, its case against, and its sources.

It does not three things

  • 01Know anything you did not give it. Thin material is filed as thin.
  • 02Replace a lawyer, an accountant, or a person who signs.
  • 03Give investment or legal advice, and it does not claim to be right.

Questions

The ones asked before signing up.

What is a material set?

A material set is a group of documents the panel reads together. Everything in it carries one colour on the map and in a verdict's sources, and a decision can read more than one set. In the built product it was called a corpus; the customer-facing word is material.

How long does a decision take?

A decision argues in passes, and every pass is a round of the debate. You choose up to four. A decision that converges stops early, and the tape shows which pass did it.

What is the 0.80 rule?

The argument stops when one position holds 0.80 of the weight across the last two passes. It is a threshold, not a promise: a decision that never reaches it is filed as a plurality, with the share it did reach.

What happens to my material?

It stays yours. Export the material, the decisions and the verdicts as one file at any time, or delete the account and everything in it. Nothing leaves the machine you start it on, in the built product.

Is this advice?

No. It is an analysis of the material you gave it, in the form of a panel that argues in passes. It does not know anything you did not give it, and it does not claim to be right.

Method

How a panel is checked.

A plain account of how a verdict is made, and what it does not claim.

The experts
Each expert is drawn from your own material. Nothing is kept in a store of experts, and a decision's panel is that decision's panel. If the material does not answer the question, no panel is seated and the run says so.
The pairings
Experts are paired by what their documents have in common, so the argument happens between the people who actually disagree about the material.
The passes
Every turn is filed with its position, its strength and its confidence. The record is not rewritten afterwards; the verdict is written from the record.
The weight
The weight of a turn comes from its strength and its confidence, so a firm, well-supported turn counts for more than a hedged one. An abstention carries no weight at all.
The threshold
The argument converges when one position holds 0.80 of the weight across the last two passes. Below that, the position with the largest share is filed as a plurality — never as a consensus, and never rounded up.
Worked example0.80

Pass 3 holds 0.82, so the argument stopped there.

What it does not claim
It is an analysis of the material you gave it, not an adviser. It knows nothing you did not give it, and it says so when the material is thin. It does not replace a lawyer, an accountant, or a person who signs.

The weight shares and the threshold above are the built product's. The worked example is illustrative.
