# Should the firm expand into Europe in 2027?

## Recommendation
Position: POSITIVE, 82%, settled
Enter the German SME segment in the second half of 2027, not before. [1]
Proceed only if the price gap to the local incumbent holds for two quarters.
The board agreed on the direction and the segment. It did not settle the launch date.
Confidence in the direction is high. Confidence in the date is low.
The answer changes if the incumbent's price cut becomes permanent.

## Timing: the SME launch depends on how long the price cut lasts
Point: a competitor is cutting SME prices in Berlin [1]. (from the documents)
Against:
The cut may be a short tactic to win first accounts, not a lasting price.
If so, waiting six months could avoid the discount entirely.
The sources do not say how long the cut will last.
So the timing argument rests on one assumption: that the cut is permanent.
A fast launch is a bet on that assumption.
Opinion: The timing argument is strong only if the cut lasts, and that is unknown.
The board did not test the assumption, so the date should wait for evidence.
Result: Track the competitor's pricing for two quarters before setting an SME date.
